# 🔥 Bantay — my cybersecurity study buddy

**Bantay** (Filipino for "guardian") is a phoenix who grows as I learn cybersecurity.
Every study sprint, quiz, and reflection helps him evolve:

🥚 Spark Egg → 🐣 Hatchling → 🛡️ Sentinel → 🦅 Guardian → 🎧 SOC Defender

## Why I built this
I'm on my path to becoming a SOC analyst in the Philippines 🇵🇭
I learn best when it's fun, so I turned studying into a quest with a buddy who grows with me.

## What's inside
- ⏱️ Focus sprints (15 and 50 minutes) and a daily streak
- 🛡️ Scam Shield, where I practice spotting real-world scams
- 🗺️ A Quest Map of my roadmap milestones
- 📝 A journal and teach-backs for reflection
- 👗 Bantay's wardrobe with 8 painted accessories earned through milestones
- 💌 Letters from Bantay every time he evolves
- 📴 Works offline, and my progress stays on my device
